Open Nova .scfg check

Needs serve.ps1 and the fixtures in app/out/scfg/: sample.scfg (a copy of Samples\SCR\System Config Test.scfg), rx*.rcfgx (copies of real receiving-card files) and shows.json (names of .scr files in app/samples/). Without them (a checkout) only the refusals run; the rest is SKIPPED. Writes what it builds to app/out/scfg/gen/ (for the NovaLCT harness).
