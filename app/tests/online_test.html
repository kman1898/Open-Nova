Online: only connected processors

An online project shows only the processors that answer now (app-online.js olConn): one that doesn't answer is left out of the tree, Device's cards, the header's scope and Monitor's counts, listed once in Device's "not connected" fold, not polled, and back as soon as it answers. Nothing connected: the empty state, nothing alarming. Offline: everything. The helper is stubbed (DRV.post): nothing leaves the page.
